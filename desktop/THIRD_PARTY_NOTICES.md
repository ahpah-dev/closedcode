ClosedCode's original application code is dedicated to the public domain under the Unlicense. Bundled third-party components keep their own licenses; the public-domain dedication does not replace them.

- Electron is distributed under the MIT license, with Chromium and other third-party notices in its distribution.
- Node.js and its bundled npm/npx include their own licenses and notices in the bundled `runtime` directory (`LICENSE`, npm package licenses, and dependency notices).
- The renderer bundles React/React DOM and Lucide icons under their respective open-source licenses. Other included dependencies retain the licenses shipped in their source packages.

OmniRoute is an optional separately downloaded package. It is not preinstalled or bundled with provider credentials or model weights. Provider account terms, model licenses and quotas are separate from the application's license.
